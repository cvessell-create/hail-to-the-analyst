Encounter game tree and mixed strategies

      
      
      
      
      
Use a 2x2 matrix for the playable mixed encounter. Nash output includes pure equilibria and, when nondegenerate, one interior mixed equilibrium; it is not an exhaustive solver for degenerate games.

      
      

      
      
      

      
In this tree the NPC sees the player's move. That changes the information structure; it is not the simultaneous mixed game. Outcomes are rational only relative to the declared utilities and information.

    

    

      
Local multiplayer and fair loot

      
      
      
      
      
Defeat the counterattack NPC squad together to unlock one reward cache. Aim with the arrows; hold Space or Fire. Values for relay charge, armour reserve and ammo reserve. AW: exactly 100 points. Knaster: total within your available fictional credits.

      
      
      
      

      
Power auction: use Relay as the single bid. Highest bid wins and pays second-highest; ties use join order. One power cell per arena: +5 shot damage and +1 level. Credits plus treasury are conserved.
